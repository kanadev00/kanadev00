Problem

Restaurants using paper or PDF menus struggled to keep up with digital transformation — updating menus was slow, costly, and prone to errors. For diners in Vancouver's diverse, multicultural food scene, navigating unfamiliar menus without dietary context created friction at every meal.

There was no B2B/B2C solution that simultaneously made menu management effortless for restaurant owners while giving diners the confidence to explore and order.

My Contributions

As Product Manager: Partnered with designers to define feature requirements and UI specifications. Optimized user experience to minimize onboarding friction for both B2B (restaurant owners) and B2C (diners) users. Created a strategic business plan for future scalability.

As Developer: Developed the customer menu interface with a favorites system and Google Maps integration. Built the PWA-ready frontend with real-time Firebase data sync, dietary filters, and QR-accessible menu views.

Impact

Delivered a working B2B/B2C Progressive Web App that streamlines dining and menu management with real-time updates, dietary filters, and QR-accessible menus — helping diners eat with confidence and restaurants manage with ease.

Recognized with the Best Web App Product Award, issued by Langara College (Aug 2025 Project1).
