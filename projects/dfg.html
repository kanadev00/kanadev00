Problem

The website served two distinct audiences — volunteers and non-profit organizations — but lacked clear messaging and separate user flows for each. Content was text-heavy, making it difficult to grasp the core message at a glance. Supporting imagery was generic and disconnected from the content, failing to reinforce the organization's mission.

Solution & Contributions

  • Interviewed the Customer Development Lead to clarify the primary target audience and define the desired user actions, then redesigned the information architecture and user flows accordingly.
  • Created visuals aligned with the content to help users absorb information quickly and intuitively.
  • Reduced copy to the minimum necessary, lowering the risk of drop-off before users reached a call to action.

Impact

Delivered a full website renewal that increased inquiries by 4.5x.
